# Initial Development Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
Finalize naming, domain, trademarks review, logo system, brand guidelines, editorial charter and corporate architecture.

## Phase 2 — Platform
Design and develop the luxury digital publishing platform, CMS, edition architecture, localization and global content model.

## Phase 3 — Editorial Launch
Establish initial editorial team, launch cover story, core departments, newsletter, video and social distribution.

## Phase 4 — Initial Editions
Launch selected pilot country/community editions and test syndication between local and global desks.

## Phase 5 — Signature Properties
Launch HIGH PROFILE 100, Films, Podcast, Events and Private.

## Phase 6 — International Scale
Expand bureaus, languages, print editions, partnerships, events and selected national operations.
