# Brand Identity

## Master Brand
HIGH PROFILE

## Descriptor
Magazine

## Primary Tagline
**THE WORLD, IN PROFILE.**

## Brand Promise
**Global Prestige. Local Authenticity. Exceptional Stories.**

## Personality
- Luxurious
- Global
- Sophisticated
- Authoritative
- Cinematic
- Modern
- Selective
- Culturally aware

## Visual Direction
The visual system should support:
- Black over white
- White over black
- Red over black
- Red over white
- Premium metallic treatments for special applications

## Logo System
The evolving identity includes an HP globe monogram representing the worldwide network, paired with a strong HIGH PROFILE / HI PROFILE magazine wordmark.

Final production assets should include vector master files, monochrome variants, favicon/app mark, horizontal/vertical lockups and print-safe versions.

## Photography
Cinematic portraits, luxury architecture, international culture, premium fashion, business leaders, artists and carefully art-directed documentary photography.
