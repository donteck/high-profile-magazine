## Example
HIGH PROFILE → North America → USA → Massachusetts → Boston → Community Edition

## Edition Principle
Every edition follows one HIGH PROFILE identity and editorial standard while remaining locally authentic.

## Global Syndication
Local Story → National Recognition → Regional Exposure → Global Platform

Strong local stories can be elevated into regional and HIGH PROFILE Global coverage.

## Country Bureau Model
A mature bureau may include:
- Editor-in-Chief
- Managing Editor
- Business Editor
- Culture Editor
- Music Editor
- Style/Fashion Editor
- Photographers
- Videographers
- Correspondents
- Partnerships / Advertising Director

Bureaus can grow into full national editions as their audience and operations mature.
