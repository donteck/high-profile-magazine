# Revenue Model

HIGH PROFILE should build diversified revenue rather than depend on a single source.

## Revenue Channels
- Premium advertising
- Brand partnerships
- Sponsorships
- Subscriptions
- HIGH PROFILE Private membership
- Events
- Original productions
- Studio services
- Licensing
- Special print editions
- Digital products
- Newsletter sponsorship
- Clearly disclosed branded content
- Strategic partnerships

## Principle
Commercial relationships must not be presented as independent editorial judgment. Sponsored and branded content should be clearly labeled.
